Detector

A tap fires when the high-passed gyro twist goes over the trigger line: sensitivity × idle noise, never below the floor.

Tap the screen gently with a fingertip pad (or the lid back). Never press hard. A light knuckle tap near the top or side edges is the safer fallback.

    Calibration

    Zones are visited in shuffled blocks so drift spreads evenly. Calibration resumes where you left off; the model retrains when it finishes.

    Not-a-tap examples

    Record 30 s of things that should not count: desk knocks, adjusting the lid, picking the laptop up. They become a “noise” class the model learns to reject.

    Cross-validated accuracy

    Each tap is scored by a model that never saw it (stratified k-fold). Merged layouts show what you'd get if neighbouring zones were combined; pick the finest layout that is reliably high.

    Learning curve

    Accuracy vs taps per zone used for training (rest held out).

    Confusion matrix

    Rows: zone you tapped. Columns: zone predicted. Off-diagonal heat = zones the sensor can't tell apart.

    Feature map

    Every recorded tap projected to 2-D (PCA). Tight, separate clouds mean separable zones.

    Zone signatures

    Average strength-normalised gyro twist per zone (x, y, z). Physics predicts left vs right flips the sign of one axis, top vs bottom changes the ratios.
